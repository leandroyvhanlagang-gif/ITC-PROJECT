Hi, I’m Leandro.

I’m a first-year Computer Science student at Ateneo de Davao University. CodeNest brings together my JavaScript exercises for Intro to Computing.

Purpose of this project

Each exercise tackles something different: converting units, choosing a tax bracket, repeating a calculation, or keeping a list of employees. This project is my way of putting those lessons into practice.

  • ⇄ Conversion Calculator: practice with functions, formulas and input checking.
  • ₱ Income Tax Calculator: practice with conditions and tax brackets.
  • ⟳ Looping Exercises: practice with while, do-while and for loops.
  • ▤ Payroll Management: practice with arrays, objects and updating the page.
